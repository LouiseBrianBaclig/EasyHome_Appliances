Our Story

Making essential living
accessible for all Filipinos.

EasyHome Appliances was founded with a simple belief: every Filipino family deserves access to quality home appliances — regardless of income level.

Our Mission

Bridging the gap between need and access

We observed that millions of Filipinos — from low-income families to students to small business owners — were unable to access basic home appliances simply because of the high one-time cost of purchase.

EasyHome bridges this gap with an innovative installment and rental model, making it possible to bring home a refrigerator, washing machine, aircon, or TV with zero or minimal down payment and manageable monthly dues.
