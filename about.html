FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EasyHome.

What are the requirements to apply? +
You only need a valid government-issued ID, proof of income (payslip, certificate of employment, or business permit), and a Philippine mobile number. No bank account required.
Is there a down payment required? +
Most of our plans have zero or very low down payment options. Some premium items may require a small down payment, which will be displayed clearly before you apply.
How long does approval take? +
Most applications are reviewed within 24–48 hours. Our team will contact you via the phone number you provided once a decision has been made.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept GCash and major bank transfers. Payment reminders are sent via SMS on or before your due date each month.
Is delivery and installation really free? +
Yes! Free delivery and professional installation are included on all approved plans, within our covered areas. We'll confirm your location during the application process.
What happens if I miss a payment? +
We understand that circumstances can change. Please contact our support team as soon as possible if you anticipate missing a payment. We will work with you to find a suitable arrangement.
